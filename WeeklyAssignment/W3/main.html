1. Classic Soft & Fluffy Cinnamon Rolls

90 mins • Yeast Dough

Ingredients

  • Dough: 4 cups all-purpose flour, 1 packet active dry yeast, 1 cup warm milk, 1/3 cup melted butter, 1/3 cup sugar, 2 eggs
  • Filling: 1/3 cup softened butter, 1 cup brown sugar, 2.5 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • Icing: 4 oz cream cheese, 1/4 cup butter, 1.5 cups powdered sugar, 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Mix warm milk, yeast, and sugar. Let bloom for 5 minutes until frothy.
  2. Add eggs, butter, and flour. Knead into a smooth dough and let rise for 1 hour until doubled in size.
  3. Roll dough into a 12x18 inch rectangle. Spread softened butter and sprinkle brown sugar and cinnamon evenly.
  4. Roll tightly into a log, slice into 12 rolls, place in a greased baking pan, and let rise for 20 minutes.
  5. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20-25 minutes until golden brown. Whisk icing ingredients together and spread over warm rolls.

2. Quick 30-Minute No-Yeast Rolls

30 mins • No Proofing

Ingredients

  • Dough: 2 cups all-purpose flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp salt, 3 tbsp cold butter, 3/4 cup milk
  • Filling: 3 tbsp melted butter, 1/2 cup brown sugar, 1.5 tbsp cinnamon
  • Glaze: 1 cup powdered sugar, 2 tbsp milk, 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Whisk dry ingredients, cut in cold butter until crumbly, then stir in milk to form a dough.
  2. Roll dough on a floured surface into a 1/4-inch thick rectangle.
  3. Brush with melted butter and sprinkle cinnamon sugar mixture evenly.
  4. Roll up, slice into 8 pieces, and place in a greased round baking dish.
  5. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 20 minutes until golden. Drizzle with vanilla glaze while warm.

3. Caramel Apple Cinnamon Rolls

45 mins • Fruit Filled

Ingredients

  • Base: 1 recipe classic or quick dough
  • Filling: 1 cup finely diced apples, 1/2 cup brown sugar, 1 tbsp cinnamon, 2 tbsp butter
  • Topping: 1/4 cup caramel sauce, 1/2 cup cream cheese frosting

Method

  1. Sauté diced apples with 1 tbsp butter for 5 minutes until slightly soft; let cool.
  2. Roll out dough, spread butter, sprinkle cinnamon sugar, and scatter cooked apples across the surface.
  3. Roll into a log, slice, and arrange in a baking pan.
  4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 22-25 minutes until cooked through.
  5. Top with cream cheese frosting and drizzle generously with warm caramel sauce before serving.
